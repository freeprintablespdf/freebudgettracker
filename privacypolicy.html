Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our Free Monthly Budget Tracker handles your information.

1. Data Collection — We Collect Nothing

Our budget tracker is 100% client-side. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your financial data, income, expenses, or budget categories to any server.

All data you enter is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and remains on your device. If you clear your browser cache, your data will be deleted. We do not require account creation, so we do not collect your name, email, or bank details.

2. Cookies and LocalStorage

We use browser localStorage to save your budget so you can see it next time you visit. This is not a tracking cookie. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following to run our website:

  • GitHub Pages: For hosting. Subject to GitHub's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics / Google Search Console (Optional): We may use anonymized analytics to see which features are popular. No personal or financial data is sent.
  • Google AdSense (If you use ads): May use cookies to show relevant ads.

We do not sell your data.

4. Children's Privacy

This tool is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

5. Your Rights

Since we store nothing on our servers, there is nothing to request for deletion. You can delete your budget data at any time by clicking "Clear All Data" in the app or clearing your browser storage.

If you have questions, contact us at your-email@gmail.com.
