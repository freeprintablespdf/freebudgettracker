About Our Monthly Budget Tracker

We built this free monthly budget tracker because budgeting shouldn't be complicated or expensive.

Most budget apps like YNAB, Mint, or EveryDollar require sign-ups, bank connections, and monthly fees. We wanted something simpler — a tool that anyone can open, use in 2 minutes, and close without worrying about their data.

Our Mission

To help 100,000+ people take control of their monthly income and expenses without selling their financial data.

Why is it different?

  • Privacy First: Most trackers store your financial data on their servers. Ours doesn't. Everything is saved in your browser's localStorage.
  • No Login: No email, no password. No spam.
  • Truly Free: No premium version. We run on minimal ads to keep hosting free.

This project is maintained by an independent developer and is open-source on GitHub Pages. If you find it useful, please share it.

Who is this tool for?

Students, families, low-income households, freelancers, and anyone who wants to follow a 50/30/20 or zero-based budgeting method.
